# Visual reference attribution

The montages `style_references.png` and `style_appraisal.png` include resized reference figures from the installed Skill gallery, labelled and arranged for design comparison. None of their original data enter the GASF analysis. Formal GASF figures are drawn from the supplied GASF logs, using only selected visual arrangements. Attribution below is transcribed from the installed gallery's THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

- `oa-tech-pone-0258309-f04`, Figure 4. © 2021 Zita Iloskics, Tamás Sebestyén and Erik Braun. [Shock propagation channels behind the global economic contagion network. The role of economic sectors and the direction of trade](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0258309). [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Used for boxes with observed points and restrained whitespace.
- `oa-geo-hess-30-629-2026-f12`, Figure 12. © 2026 Manuel Álvarez Chaves, Eduardo Acuña Espinoza, Uwe Ehret and Anneli Guthke. [When physics gets in the way: an entropy-based evaluation of conceptual constraints in hybrid hydrological models](https://hess.copernicus.org/articles/30/629/2026/). [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Used for the ECDF and paired-panel idea; the density alternative was not selected for the GASF data.
- `oa-geo-esd-15-527-2024-f04`, Figure 4. © 2024 Federico Fabiano, Paolo Davini, Virna L. Meccia, Giuseppe Zappa, Alessio Bellucci, Valerio Lembo, Katinka Bellomo and Susanna Corti. [Multi-centennial evolution of the climate response and deep-ocean heat uptake in a set of abrupt stabilization scenarios with EC-Earth3](https://esd.copernicus.org/articles/15/527/2024/). [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Used for paired response plots with consistent category encoding.

The reference cards' caveats were retained in the adaptation: box whiskers are not confidence intervals; different panels may answer different questions; an association axis is not time, and its slope is not a temporal rate. No Skill/gallery files were modified or republished as an entire collection.
